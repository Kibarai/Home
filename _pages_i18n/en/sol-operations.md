      - title: Automatic classification on arrival
        body: >-
          Every ticket, wherever it comes from, arrives already carrying its
          category, its priority and the product affected. Priority comes from
          what the ticket says, not from what the customer ticked. Consistent
          labelling from the first minute.
      - title: Drafts from your knowledge base
        body: >-
          For the questions asked again and again, the AI writes the draft from
          your documentation and your past resolutions. The agent reviews and
          sends. What gets corrected feeds back into the system.
      - title: Real-time SLA monitoring
        body: >-
          The queue is ordered by risk of breach rather than arrival time, with
          a warning before anything slips and automatic escalation if something
          urgent stalls.
      - title: Duplicate and pattern detection
        body: >-
          If several tickets with the same root arrive within minutes, the
          system groups them and flags a major incident, instead of five people
          working the same thing separately.
