  - type: soluciones
    title: What we can automate in your company.
    subtitle: >-
      Five areas, and in each one the detail of what stops being done by hand.
      You do not have to start with all of them: the diagnostic decides which
      one pays off soonest.
    items:
      - title: Rotas and planning
        body: >-
          The rota stops being rebuilt from scratch every time something moves.
        bullets:
          - Shifts and rest periods against the real rules of your agreement and each contract
          - Automatic recalculation when an absence, an incident or extra cover comes in
          - Cover proposed from among those who can actually do it, instead of ringing round blind
          - Hours per person against those contracted, visible during the month and not at the end
          - A rota per contract and a rota per person, built from the same data
      - title: Service reports and patrol rounds
        body: >-
          What happens on shift is documented as it happens.
        bullets:
          - Report logged from a phone, with time and location
          - Checkpoint marked where and when it should be, not reconstructed at the end of the shift
          - Incidents with photos, tied to their service and their client
          - Signed off by the supervisor without passing through paper
          - Works without signal and uploads what is pending as soon as it returns
      - title: Regulatory compliance
        body: >-
          What expires stops being tracked on a separate spreadsheet, and stops
          being discovered late.
        bullets:
          - Each officer's professional licence tracked, with a warning before it expires
          - Annual refresher training — who is up to date and whose turn it is now
          - Licences and specialisms per person, so you know who can cover which service
          - A check on assignment — if something is missing, you are warned before they go on shift
          - Every item with its supporting document filed and findable when it has to be shown
      - title: Document control
        body: >-
          Paperwork stops being chased by email and stops living in folders only
          one person can navigate.
        bullets:
          - Documentation by person, by contract and for the company itself, in one place
          - Service contracts and their amendments, with the full history
          - Validity tracked, with a warning before anything expires
          - Findable immediately, without digging through folders or email
      - title: Per-service billing
        body: >-
          The invoice comes out of what was delivered, not out of reconciling
          the month by hand.
        bullets:
          - By the hour or by post, according to what each contract says
          - Night, weekend and armed supplements passed through when they apply, with no manual review
          - Overtime, extra cover and one-off services outside the rota, billed and not forgotten
          - Services delivered and never billed, in plain sight instead of lost
          - Each client sees the detail of what they are charged and why
